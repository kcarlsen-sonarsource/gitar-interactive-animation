01 · high-signal review

High signal. Very little noise.

Gitar reviews every pull request with full context of your codebase and conventions, and is tuned to flag issues that are real. Fewer false positives means a higher fix rate, less review fatigue, and fewer tokens spent chasing ghosts.

  • real findings surface, nitpicks dissolve
  • one consolidated comment

02 · ci analysis

CI failures diagnosed, not dumped on you.

Gitar reads your pipeline and separates real breakage from flaky tests and infrastructure noise. It de-duplicates failures and root-causes what actually went wrong, so nobody loses an afternoon to logs.

  • flaky test

03 · gitar fixes

Fixes applied autonomously — under your supervision.

When Gitar finds a problem it doesn't just comment. It generates a real fix and commits it to the branch, within the rules you define. You decide what it can fix, what it must escalate, and what blocks a merge.
